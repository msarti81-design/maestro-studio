Privacy Policy

Application: VirtuKeys Studio (it.freeserv.virtukeys) Effective Date: November 5, 2026 Developer: FreeServ / Marco Sarti

Summary: VirtuKeys Studio respects your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, and audio recording / MIDI creation happens locally on your device under your explicit control.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the VirtuKeys Studio application (including the Android package it.freeserv.virtukeys and associated web applications). It explains how we handle information when you use our interactive grand piano, sheet music composer, jam band studio, and multiplayer practice features.

2. Information We Collect and Process

VirtuKeys Studio is designed with a privacy-by-default architecture:

  • Audio & Microphone Data: If you use audio recording features, microphone input is accessed solely in real-time to capture your musical performance. We do not run background listening services. Audio files are saved directly onto your device storage only when you tap Save/Export.
  • Storage Access (WAV & MIDI): The app requests storage permissions strictly to export your composed sheet music, MIDI files (.mid), and audio tracks (.wav) to your device's download/media folder.
  • Anonymous Session Identifiers: In multiplayer jam rooms or masterclasses, the app uses a temporary, user-selected nickname and room code to broadcast real-time musical note events (e.g., Note On, Note Off, pitch) to other participants in the same room. No real names, emails, or personal contacts are required.
  • Local Preferences: App preferences (such as keyboard layout, sound preset, volume levels, and visual themes) are stored locally on your device via standard local storage.

3. How We Use the Information

Any technical data processed by the application is used strictly for:

  • Synthesizing low-latency musical audio and rendering visual musical notes.
  • Allowing you to save and export your own musical recordings.
  • Enabling synchronized real-time interactive jamming with other connected students or musicians in private rooms.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

VirtuKeys Studio does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral profiling trackers, or data brokers. Network communications are limited to:

  • Connecting to our secure WebSocket / HTTP service for multi-user jam rooms and version synchronization.
  • Retrieving static application assets over secure HTTPS.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

VirtuKeys Studio is an educational, creative musical application designed for users of all ages, including music students and children. We comply fully with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • We do not collect, request, or store personally identifiable information (PII) from children.
  • Multiplayer features use non-identifying musical rooms with no open personal profiling.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that any personal information has been inadvertently transmitted, please contact us immediately to have it purged.

6. Data Security and Retention

Your musical creations (recorded songs, composed scores) remain your intellectual property and reside primarily on your local device. Temporary real-time musical event packets transmitted during multiplayer jams are ephemeral and are not stored permanently after the session ends.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data is processed (we do not store personal profiles).
  • Request deletion of any temporary room nicknames or cached records.
  • Revoke device permissions (Microphone, Storage) at any time through your Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > VirtuKeys > Permissions).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated Effective Date.
